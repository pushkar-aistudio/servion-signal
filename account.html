Research pack

Why this account. Carle Health runs a multi-site patient access operation across Illinois with roughly 640 contact-centre seats on an on-prem Genesys estate. The speech platform underneath it has a published end-of-support date, which is the dated pressure the score keys on.

What they said publicly. A 2026 capital plan names patient access and digital front door as funded programmes. Two job postings in the last quarter ask for contact-centre engineers with migration experience.

Who decides. Scott Hargus owns support services and telecom. A VP of Patient Experience was appointed in September, which is the second Tier 1 persona at the account and the reason for the persona boost.

What to open with. The end-of-support date, not the platform. They have already decided to move; the question is who runs it.
