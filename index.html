1What Signal is

Signal is an intelligence layer for outbound. It decides who to engage, with what message, and when. It uses Servion's existing methods and data, pulled into one place rather than replaced.

Signal connects to Servion's data sources, prioritises the account lists Servion already builds, and finds new accounts that look like past wins, automatically skipping existing customers and anything already in the pipeline. For each one, it identifies the right stakeholders with verified email and phone, builds the account research, and drafts the outreach for the SDR to review and send.

Starting point: North America, banking and healthcare, one SDR, through to SAL. Extendable to further verticals once the model is proven.

4Scope

1Sourcing &
prioritisation
  • Salesforce records, including the ZoomInfo lists synced into it
  • Lookalike accounts modelled on past wins
  • Existing customers and live opportunities excluded automatically
  • Account and contact-level fit scoring, configurable to Servion's criteria, engagement score as one input
Does not
  • Create new Accounts in Salesforce. Signal writes Leads and activity; new Accounts follow Servion's approval process.
›
2Enrichment &
context
  • Account enriched through ZoomInfo, Clay and Apollo
  • Contacts identified and verified against Servion's persona set, ranked on role fit and data completeness
  • Research pack per account surfaced
Does not
  • Contact anyone. Every message is reviewed and sent by a person.
›
3Outreach
  • Personalised email, call script and LinkedIn drafts on Servion's own templates, matched to the account's campaign
  • Drafts placed with the SDR to review and send
  • Real-time notification on high-sentiment replies
  • Write-back: lead, activity and a Signal source tag into Salesforce
Does not
  • Send in bulk. Mass campaign execution stays with Phave.
  • Place calls or book meetings automatically
  • Capture or analyse call recordings
Sales command centre
  • Every account in one place: campaign history, Signal activity, outreach to date, and the AE's fit score once it returns
  • Executive view of activity and outcomes against the agreed measures, tracked from baseline
  • Accounts that have gone quiet between steps, flagged for follow-up
Does not
  • Score or qualify a fit call. The AE's score stays human.
  • Track an account beyond SAL
